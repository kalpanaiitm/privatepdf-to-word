# Privacy notice

PrivatePDF to Word is designed to process documents without intentionally persisting them.

## Processing

The uploaded PDF and generated Word document are held in application memory for the active session. The application code does not write document bytes, filenames, or extracted text to a database, analytics service, or permanent filesystem location.

The **Clear this session** control deletes conversion results held by the application and resets the upload widget. Closing or expiring the session also releases application-held objects.

## Important limitation

The hosting platform and network infrastructure may process operational metadata or logs outside this application's direct control. Therefore, do not upload highly sensitive, secret, regulated, unpublished, legally privileged, or export-controlled documents.

## User responsibility

Upload only material you own or are authorised to convert. Download and inspect the Word document before relying on it. Conversion may alter layout or omit content.
